Frequently Asked Questions

Get answers to common questions about our web design services

How long does it take to complete a website?

Project timelines vary based on complexity:

  • Starter Plan (1 page): 2-3 business days
  • Professional Plan (up to 3 pages): 5-7 business days
  • Enterprise Plan (up to 10 pages): 10-14 business days

We'll provide you with a detailed timeline after discussing your specific requirements.

What's included in each pricing plan?

Each plan includes different features:

  • Free Plan: 1 responsive page, basic HTML/CSS, contact form
  • Professional Plan (€29.95): Up to 3 pages, HTML/CSS/JavaScript, interactive elements, SEO optimization
  • Enterprise Plan (€59.95): Up to 10 pages, advanced JavaScript, custom animations, priority support

All plans include responsive design and cross-browser compatibility.

Do I need to provide content and images?

Yes, you'll need to provide:

  • Text content for all pages
  • Images, logos, and graphics
  • Brand colors and style preferences
  • Any specific functionality requirements

We can provide guidance on image specifications and content structure to help you prepare.

Will my website work on mobile devices?

Absolutely! All our websites are built with a mobile-first approach:

  • Responsive design that adapts to all screen sizes
  • Touch-friendly navigation and buttons
  • Optimized loading speeds on mobile networks
  • Cross-browser compatibility (Chrome, Safari, Firefox, Edge)

Your website will look and function perfectly on smartphones, tablets, and desktops.

Can I make changes to my website after it's completed?

Yes, we provide several options for updates:

  • Minor text changes: Free for the first 30 days
  • Content updates: €15 per hour after the initial period
  • New features: Quoted based on complexity
  • Training: We can teach you basic HTML editing

We'll also provide you with the source code so you can make changes yourself if preferred.

What about SEO and search engine visibility?

Professional and Enterprise plans include basic SEO optimization:

  • Semantic HTML structure for better search engine understanding
  • Meta tags and descriptions
  • Optimized page loading speeds
  • Mobile-friendly design (Google ranking factor)
  • Clean, crawlable code structure

For advanced SEO services, we can discuss additional options based on your needs.

Do I need the hosting service, or can I use my own?

You have complete flexibility:

  • Use our hosting: €2.50/month with SSL, support, and easy setup
  • Use your own hosting: We'll provide all files for easy upload
  • Popular alternatives: Works with Netlify, Vercel, GitHub Pages, etc.
  • Setup help: We can assist with deployment on your chosen platform

Our hosting is optional but convenient for those who want a hassle-free solution.

What if I'm not satisfied with the final result?

Your satisfaction is our priority:

  • Revision rounds: 2 rounds of revisions included in all paid plans
  • Clear communication: We'll share progress updates and mockups
  • Satisfaction guarantee: If you're not happy, we'll work until you are
  • Refund policy: Full refund if we can't meet your requirements

We believe in building long-term relationships with our clients through quality work.
